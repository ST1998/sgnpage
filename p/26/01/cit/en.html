Names in 🇮🇹 for the classic triad (Crash B.)

🧬 Context

Prior to Crash Team Racing (1999) and Crash Bandicoot: The Wrath of Cortex (2001), the level names in earlier games were provided in at most two languages: American English & Japanese. The rest of the world simply defaulted to the 1st choice - a practice that could lead to confusion, as certain names relied on puns or double meanings that might not be understood.

Since not even the N. Sane Trilogy (the 2017 remake) deigned to do this (for faithfulness), my mind got involved in remedying it, imagining (for fun) that I was in the creative translation department (specifically, linguistic adaptation).

As of Oct 18, 2026, to celebrate the re-release and new edition of this article, I’ve decided to add Crash Bash to the list!
