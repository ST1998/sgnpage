Information Hub 🇶 What is the "sgnpage" logo for?
🇦 Beyond serving as a cute signature, it's also situationable interactive; tapping it produces different results:
• In the "Entrance", it does nothing
• In the "Atrium", it takes you back to the "Entrance"
• Everywhere else, it takes you back to the "Atrium"

🇶 Is it possible to change the language without having to go through the "Entrance"?
🇦 Certainly! Simply change "en.html" to "it.html" in your web browser's address bar. You can also do this if you only have the link in text format
