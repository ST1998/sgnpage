Questions & Answers 🇶 What is the "sgnpage" logo for?
🇦 Beyond serving as a cute signature, it is also interactive; tapping it produces different results depending on the situation:
• In the "Entrance", it does nothing
• In the "Atrium", it takes you back to the "Entrance"
• Everywhere else, it takes you back to the "Atrium"

🇶 Is it possible to change the language without having to go through the "Entrance"?
🇦 Certainly! Simply change "en.html" to "it.html" in your web browser's address bar. You can also do this if you only have the link in text format

🇶 Is this version optimized for text-based browsers?
🇦 Especially for those! PHP, JavaScript, TypeScript... what are those things? There’s no room for them here! In any case... the limitations - if your text-based browser doesn't account for them - are that it only loads the system font, images won't load and CSS won't work
