Idea on extra elemental shield (Sonic)

Idea on extra elemental shield (Sonic): Scudorang / Scudo



NOTE: Fire button: A / B / C on the SEGA Genesis / Mega Drive joypad

( 0 ) Useful as a throwing weapon, especially in boss battles, get rings, hit monitors 'n' badniks and press buttons from a distance; at high speed while running it can be a problem to use, unless you know the layout of the zone well
( 1 ) Normally the shield throw goes where the character is facing forward; if after pressing the fire button a twice time you quickly press a direction button, you'll throw it in the direction of interest up to 4 distinct ones (up, right, down, left)
( 2 ) You'll be vulnerable if the shield is still active with the attack and retreat; if you get hit, you'll lose the shield (it'll go off the rails) and take damage (if you were still in mid-air, gravity will reset). Use it wisely!
( 3 ) If you throw it away while you're in mid-air, you'll be stuck there until the shield returns to you, resetting the gravity previously to 0. You can only throw it once and can only do it again if you hit the land (excluding monitors, badniks, springs)
( 4 ) Stand on the water at all times (as long as you have it on); depending on the momentum of the fall before entering there, the dive will be greater before the rapid return to the surface and, out of there, you'll get a hi-loaded bounce
( 5 ) The speed and distance the shield will cover during the throw will vary based on the character's movement speed, which can even exit the screen to significantly anticipate what will be waiting for you further ahead
( 6 ) If the shield is hit by fire, ice or blades, its attack will be nullified, as well as losing it forever. Unavailable in warmer and colder climates
( 7 ) During its attack and retreat state, it can hit multiple monitors and badniks at once as long as they are facing the same direction as the shield
